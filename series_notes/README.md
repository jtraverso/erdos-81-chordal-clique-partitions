# Series notes — Preprint 1.0

Juan Pablo Traverso Gianini · 18 October 2026

These notes have independent manuscripts, formal projects and validation evidence.
They connect the established results of Paper IV with the transfer framework of V
and the local exact formulas of VI.

- [Note I: From realizability certificates to the Erdős 81 bound](NOTE_I_ERDOS81_TRANSFER/): an attributed reorganization of part of IV through V. It does not claim an independent new solution of the problem.
- [Note II: Exact stability for nearly extremal chordal clique partitions](NOTE_II_CHORDAL_STABILITY/): a quantitative application of IV and VI in its stated deficit window.

Each package includes English and Spanish manuscripts and formalization companions
in Markdown, LaTeX and PDF, a bilingual HTML explanation, Lean source archives,
finite arithmetic checks and licenses.
