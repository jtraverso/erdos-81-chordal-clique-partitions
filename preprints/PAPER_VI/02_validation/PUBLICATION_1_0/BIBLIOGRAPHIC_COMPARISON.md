# Paper VI Preprint 1.0 — scope of the bibliographic comparison

This is a focused comparison of the stated results, not a certificate of exhaustive historical priority. It uses the source inspection recorded for the current manuscript. No third-party full article is redistributed in this dossier.

| Result group | Relevant antecedent and limited comparison |
|---|---|
| Universal lower bound and efficient K4/K5 types | Erdős–Faudree–Ordman 88 supplies the lower-bound framework and efficient block types. These are credited antecedents. The local construction attaining equality under simultaneous bounded-degree core deletions, crossing deletions and exterior additions is the manuscript's separate assertion. |
| Independent-join boundary | Wallis–Zhang93 Proposition 3.2 and Corollary 3.3 identify the exact join criterion through proper edge coloring. The boundary of D with b=q−1 falls within that classical result under its coloring condition; it is not presented as a new formula. |
| General split and chordal background | Chen–Erdős–Ordman94 and Erdős–Ordman–Zalcstein93 provide the split/chordal setting. Wallis–Wu91 is compared only through its primary abstract and author announcement. The local theorem does not assert that every edited graph is split or chordal. |
| Triangle restriction B | Okechukwu26 Lemma 4.2 gives a triangle-partition upper bound under its own coloring, capacity and defect conditions. Agreement is asserted only in matching clean-crossing subcases. It is not claimed to supply the full simultaneous-edit window of B. |
| Exact join constructions | Ning26 Proposition 3.1 is a relevant equality construction in a family of joins of unions of cliques. That antecedent prevents attributing novelty to the mere use of K4s or equality in a signed lower bound. |
| Exceptional family D | Ma88 is relevant threshold-graph background in the split subfamily; it does not identify the whole partially adjacent exceptional family as threshold. The arbitrary a,b exceptional model has its stated q≥p+3 condition. |
| Counting E | McGuinness–Rees90 is an earlier enumeration result for line graphs. Paper VI counts labeled edge-clique partitions of its local hosts, with prescribed exterior blocks. Different coloring labels are not silently treated as distinct uncolored partitions. |
| Restricted recourse and connectivity | Earlier list-edge-coloring reconfiguration is relevant context. Section 10 concerns actual edge-clique partitions in the complete-join, bounded-load all-K5 class. It makes no unrestricted mixed-block connectivity or priority assertion. |

## Access and attribution limits

Full-text inspection covered EFO88, CEO94, the EOZ93 author galley, Ma88, MR90 and the user-supplied WZ93 article. The original Pullman–Donald81 article was not inspected. Its relevant result is credited only through EFO88 p. 94 and WZ93 Corollary 3.3. The unavailable Monson–Pullman–Rees95 survey is not used as a substantive source in the manuscript. The access limitation is disclosed instead of filling it with an assumed statement. The published manuscript retains the exact source scope rather than asserting that every bibliographic question is settled.
