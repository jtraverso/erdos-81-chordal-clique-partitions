# Paper III — Preprint v1.6

**Linear-Error Clique Partitions of Split Graphs via Structured Triangle Packing**

Juan Pablo Traverso Gianini · 19 October 2026

A structured triangle packing gives the sharp quadratic coefficient with linear error for split graphs. The resulting partition uses only triangles and single edges.

## Place in the series / Lugar en la serie

Papers I–II study fractional profiles and extremizers; III gives an integral split-graph construction; IV establishes chordal bounds and structural stability. V develops the general transfer framework, and VI studies exact local clique partitions. The proof inputs of this paper are unchanged. The triangle-packing theorem, corridor estimates and all mathematical appendices remain in the article. The discussion now distinguishes the later chordal bounds of IV from the separate linear triangle-integrality-gap question. Computational audit descriptions and declaration lists move to the companion.

La edición actual conserva los resultados y las pruebas, actualiza las relaciones con los artículos posteriores y separa la documentación formal. El artículo matemático y el complemento están disponibles en ambos idiomas.
